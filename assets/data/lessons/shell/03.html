Typing is not the skill. Navigation is. These are the shortcuts worth making automatic:

  • Tab — complete a command, path, or argument. Press twice to list possibilities. Use it constantly.
  • ↑ / ↓ — walk through command history.
  • Ctrl+R — reverse-search history. Type a fragment of an old command.
  • Ctrl+A / Ctrl+E — jump to the beginning or end of the line.
  • Ctrl+U / Ctrl+K — delete left or right of the cursor.
  • Ctrl+C — interrupt the foreground process. This is not "close the terminal"; it sends SIGINT.
  • Ctrl+L — clear the screen while keeping your command line.

history shows numbered commands. !! re-runs the last command, and sudo !! is the classic "I forgot sudo" recovery. Be careful with history expansion: confirm what !! will run before pressing Enter, especially after a destructive command.

Use clear when output is noisy, but do not confuse a clean screen with a fresh shell. The scrollback is still there, and your environment has not changed.
