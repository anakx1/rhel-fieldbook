Whichever scheduler you use, test the payload manually first, make it exit non-zero on failure, and keep evidence. A scheduled job that never ran is harder to spot than one that failed loudly.

RHEL 9 vs RHEL 10

cronie and crontab behave the same on both releases. RHEL 10 still supports cron; timers remain the better default for system services that need supervision and journal evidence.
